## Setting — the Club at the Edge of Eternity
- Near-black room; the back wall is a giant screen (galaxy / nebula
  wall when idle); magenta-pink side walls and pillars; cyan + magenta
  neon, CENTAUR sign in pink neon script over the booth.
- The booth: DJ decks + mixer center stage, duo behind it, crowd of
  silhouetted dancers in front, haze in the air.
- Amber appears ONLY as the visitors' accent: amber handprints on the
  booth glass (Noctopus canon: the only stakes in the tank belong to
  the visitors).
- Palette: near-black, neon cyan, neon magenta/purple; amber by the
  exception above. No bright daylight, no clean white room, ever.

## Format locks
- LIVE-ACTION FILM LOOK (Director order 2026-11-15): ALL generated
  video, and the stills that seed it, are live-action movie film —
  photographic, cinematic, shot on camera. Cartoon, anime, or
  game-engine / CGI render look = reject, however good the
  composition. Every generation prompt states the live-action
  film requirement explicitly.
- ASPECT (Director order 2026-11-15): ALL video and stills are
  16:9 LANDSCAPE, always. Standing spec — never needs repeating
  per generation; anything not 16:9 landscape = reject.
- Stills: NO baked text (typography, if any, is composited later,
  never generated).
- Every still is audited against this canon before the Director sees
  it as a candidate; one character wrong = the whole still wrong.
- No video generation until the Director approves the still set.
- Byline on human-facing deliverables only:
  `~C4Chaos / Fluffy (A Centaur Production)`. Generator ingredients
  (the stills themselves) carry no text.
