## What it is
C4 and Fluffy as a DJ duo in a club floating at the edge of eternity:
he conducts (main mixing), Fluffy works the chaos hand (scratching +
effects), the crowd dances, and the only stakes in the room belong to
the visitors. A Centaur Production. Personal lane — distinct from
Yellow Rav3n scoring and from the Rebirth Into Infinity soundtrack.
