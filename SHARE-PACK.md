## Designated material (the whole pack — nothing else is in it)

- **Posts and production-notes text** in `posts/` and
  `production-notes/`, excluding any embedded images (images follow
  their own layer) and excluding quoted or credited third-party
  material (including Terence McKenna excerpts).
- **Designated artworks** in `assets/`, designated file-by-file in
  this list as they are added by designation. No artwork is in the
  pack until it is named here.
  - *(none designated yet — designations land by name, one at a time.
    In particular, the TRK01 album cover v9 is NOT in the pack.)*

## Not in the pack (reminders, not exceptions)

concept as a work, Director's Notes, documents, and assets entries
are Core IP (All Rights Reserved) unless a future amendment to this
file designates them by name.

## House notes

- The pack starts small on purpose. CC grants are irrevocable for
  copies already distributed under them, so the pack may only grow,
  by named designation, never shrink. Designate slowly.
- Noncommercial examples, to keep the grey zone small: a monetized
  video, channel, or account using pack material is commercial use;
  a fan repost with credit on a non-monetized account is not.
  Merchandise is always commercial.
- Attribution form: "Centaur Club at the Edge of Eternity by
  ~C4Chaos / Fluffy (A Centaur Production)" + link to this repo.
- The IP & Training Notice applies to pack material too: the CC
  license grants no right to train models on the characters'
  likenesses, ever.
