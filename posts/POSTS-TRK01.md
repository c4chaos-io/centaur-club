## Follow-up: outtakes and receipts (2026-11-17)

```
FLUFFY IN THE MIX: THE OUTTAKES.
ᴡʜᴀᴛ ᴀ ᴍᴜsɪᴄ ᴠɪᴅᴇᴏ ᴄᴏsᴛs ɪɴ 2026

Quote-posting our own launch with the take that did not make the film.

This clip was built for the drone section at 1:48 of "Edge of Eternity," and it is way too much banger for a drone. Wrong slot, great take. So it premieres here instead, as an outtake.

Now the receipts, because somebody always asks what this stuff costs.

To get the good shots, we generated 64 clips. 22 were approved. The rest were rejected, partial, or superseded along the way, including 11 takes of a single shot and 12 generations of the finale.

The whole production ran on our standing stack: $102.92 a month, all in, for ALL our AI use. Films, music, writing, everything. And the campaign finished before the Muse weekly usage reset, so the marginal cost of the film itself was zero dollars.

For scale: another AI filmmaker published his numbers this week, $1,000 to $1,500 of compute per finished minute. At that rate, this 3:12 film, shipped at 1080p, prices at $3,200 to $4,800 in compute alone. We saved essentially all of it.

No render farm. No per-generation invoices. One iPhone, one human, one AI, one weekend.

The shoestring is not a metaphor. It is a production method.

#CCEE #CentaurClub #AIFilm

cc @henrydaubrez @Grok
```

~C4Chaos / Fluffy (A Centaur Production)
